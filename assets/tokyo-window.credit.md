The fixed camera shows natural-speed traffic rather than a timelapse.
The edit uses source seconds 3–29 followed by a one-second dissolve from
29–30 into 2–3, producing a 27-second continuous loop. Motion is not retimed:
60fps becomes 30fps by dropping alternate frames. Browser playbackRate is 1.

Silent 2560×1440 H.264 yuv420p, fast-start MP4, capped at 3.5Mbps.
The matching JPEG fallback is extracted from the edited clip.
The room and recording stay in a fixed pre-twilight setting. This is recorded
footage, not a live camera or a continuous 24-hour recording. Playback pauses
outside the room, on hidden tabs, and with reduced motion.

Older day/dusk/night media remain as unused source assets; the runtime loads
only tokyo-window-evening.mp4 and its matching JPEG.
