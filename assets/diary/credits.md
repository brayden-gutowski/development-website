# Diary material and type sources

- **Paper001** by Lennart Demes / ambientCG, CC0 1.0. `paper-fiber.jpg` is the unmodified 1K color texture from https://ambientcg.com/a/Paper001 . Source archive: https://ambientcg.com/get?file=Paper001_1K-JPG.zip . License information: https://docs.ambientcg.com/license/ . The warm paper tint is applied at runtime.
- **Book Pattern** by Rob Tuytel / Poly Haven, CC0. `paper.jpg` is the unmodified 1K page-edge texture from https://polyhaven.com/a/book_pattern . Source: https://dl.polyhaven.org/file/ph-assets/Textures/jpg/1k/book_pattern/book_pattern_page_1k.jpg . Used on the transient diary's page-block edges.
- **Klee One Regular**, Fontworks Inc., SIL Open Font License 1.1. Source https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/kleeone and https://github.com/fontworks-fonts/Klee . The complete font was losslessly repackaged from TTF to WOFF2; no glyphs or font design were changed. License retained as `KleeOne-OFL.txt`.

Each blog uses two photographs or illustrations (three when there is enough room) from the user's existing blog assets, with their captions. No images were generated and no new room props were added. The reader reuses the authored leather texture for its animated cover; its temporary binding and page-block meshes cast the lifted diary's shadows while it is open and are closed, bevelled extruded solids with curved fore-edges; the original diary meshes return to their exact original transforms.

- **ゲームを作るのが好きです** (October 23, 2026): `AA_Icon.png` and the ARCADE ADVENTURES gameplay screenshot supplied by the site owner. Copied byte-for-byte to `blog-2026-10-23-arcade-adventures-icon.png` and `blog-2026-10-23-arcade-adventures-gameplay.png` in `assets/`. No image generation or edits.
